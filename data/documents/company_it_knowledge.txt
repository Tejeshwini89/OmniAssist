# OmniAssist Employee IT Knowledge Base

## Password Reset

Employees can reset their corporate password through the Company Identity Portal.

If the password reset fails, contact the IT Service Desk.

## VPN Access

Employees working remotely must connect to the corporate VPN before accessing internal applications.

The VPN client is available through the company software portal.

If VPN authentication fails repeatedly, contact the IT Service Desk.

## Laptop Replacement

Employees with damaged or malfunctioning company laptops should create an IT support request.

The IT team will evaluate the device and determine whether repair or replacement is required.

## Leave Policy

Employees should submit planned leave requests through the HR portal.

Leave requests should be submitted at least three working days before the planned leave whenever possible.

## IT Service Desk

The IT Service Desk handles password issues, VPN problems, laptop failures, and access-related incidents.

Employees should include their employee ID and a clear description of the problem when submitting a support request.
